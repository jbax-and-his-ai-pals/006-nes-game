# Design Catalog

Ideas in this document are candidates, not committed story canon. Prefer
small, readable comedy puzzles with at least two clues and no consumable
critical items.

## Puzzle Event Seeds

1. **The Fake Maintenance Notice:** Examine the Zoo notice, compare its date
   to the clipboard at the Park hatch, then enter through the service route.
   The clue is corroboration, not a single hidden pixel.
2. **Open the Zoo Gate:** Inside the Zoo, help Keeper Nibs restore a harmless
   gate control. This opens the Park-to-Zoo surface shortcut and changes NPC
   barks.
3. **Farm Irrigation Favor:** Farmer Glorp requires an irrigation adjustment
   before lending a practical farm resource. The farm stays an ordinary rural
   detour rather than the source of the Zoo's slug infestation.
4. **The Lodging Mix-Up:** Use a completed service badge to access the Lodge
   laundry, where a guest list provides a social clue rather than a key.
5. **Comet Room Soundcheck:** The club system lacks one mundane component;
   the player can obtain it from the jukebox, arcade, or mechanic through
   multiple valid routes.
6. **Sewer Shortcut Valve:** A valve key changes water flow in one sewer room,
   opening a shorter route and exposing a previously unreachable item.
7. **Prize Counter Retrieval:** A magnet retrieves evidence from behind an
   arcade prize shelf. The clerk gives a hint if the player tries to reach by
   hand first.
8. **Bell and Bubbles:** Ringing the Chapel bell at the correct time startles
   a hidden Zoo animal; its behavior points to a maintenance lie or lab clue.
9. **Rooftop Observation:** A billboard angle reveals a code visible from the
   street. The player uses it at the Annex, with an NPC hint available.
10. **Piss Corner Rumor Loop:** A banished patron cycles from jokes to a
   useful observation once the player has evidence or a particular item.
11. **Laundromat Cycle:** Pay the washer, then the dryer, to turn one dirty
    object into its clean and dry puzzle-ready form. Failed attempts explain
    whether money, cleaning, or drying is still missing.

## Design Rules

- Every critical puzzle needs at least two discoverable clues.
- Failed actions should give a joke, a hint, or both.
- New locations should add a shortcut, an information loop, or an item loop;
  avoid one-way content islands.
- Prefer reusable tools and persistent unlocks over consumable solutions.
